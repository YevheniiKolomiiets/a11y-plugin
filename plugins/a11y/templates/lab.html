How to read. “Current implementation” shows the colours from the code. “Proposal” uses the palette on the left: agreed values, chosen decision options and your own edits (saved in this browser only). Threshold is 4.5 for text and 3.0 for large text, icons and boundaries. Translucent layers and opacity are blended onto the background before the ratio is computed. Green row: the proposal passes. Reddish: changed but still failing. Plain: unchanged and failing. Click a row, or focus its name and press Enter, to see only its tokens in the palette. Show all tokens brings the rest back. Click a token, or focus its name and press Enter, to see only the elements that use it. Show all elements brings the rest back. In an open decision, click an option to choose it for the theme you are looking at and copy the choice for the agent; other themes stay open.
